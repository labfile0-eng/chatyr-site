PILOT DATA / 1 November 2026

Data: storage
and processing.

This page describes the information needed for the website chat pilot and how it is processed. Purposes, retention, AI processing and deletion arrangements are agreed in the contract before connection.

Information and access

To discuss onboarding, send your website, business contact and the task. The chat processes visitor messages, contact details they provide and technical identifiers. Each company receives a separate account and knowledge base, with access checked before launch. The pilot is not offered for sensitive, medical or secret information. Do not send passwords, payment details or information unnecessary for the agreed task.

Storage

The main server is hosted by PS.kz in Astana, Kazakhstan. Backups on that server are in the same location. An additional encrypted copy was created manually on the service owner’s computer; its location is not confirmed. Each component’s location and access are documented before connection.

AI processing

Anthropic outside Kazakhstan receives the approved knowledge base and up to 12 selected conversation messages. Some common contact and payment-detail patterns are filtered from messages; the knowledge base is reviewed separately. This does not guarantee complete anonymisation. AI can make errors. Important replies need human review, and visitors can request an operator. AI limits and provider processing terms are agreed before connection.

Contact and channels

Send email to hello@chatyr.kz from your email application; receipt is confirmed separately. Telegram and email support channels require separate acceptance. WhatsApp and Instagram are outside the current website chat pilot.

Retention and deletion

Purposes, retention, export, deletion and backup handling are agreed in the contract before connection. For data requests, email hello@chatyr.kz and identify the business without unnecessary personal information.
